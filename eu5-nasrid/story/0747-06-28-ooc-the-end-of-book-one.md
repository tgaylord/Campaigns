Book I is written in full now. Three causes are left open in the text because the saves don't record them. Why Ibn al-Khaṭīb went over to Castile: the save only shows him as Frederick, Catholic, a Castilian admiral from 8 July 1341, so the court's three rumours are invented, and his entry should be rewritten if Trent knows the real reason. What drove stability from 34 to 7 between May 1343 and January 1345: the chronicle offers guesses and says it doesn't know. Why the burghers soured in 1341: unknown too. Also invented colour: the flowers at Ibn al-Khaṭīb's homecoming, the debate in the Jamāʿa, and the wedding in the Alhambra. The Jamāʿa's change of purpose and the marriage during it are Trent's.
