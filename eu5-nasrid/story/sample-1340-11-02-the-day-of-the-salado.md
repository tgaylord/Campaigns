Let the reader weep, for this is the record of a day on which the sword of Islam was broken upon the banks of a little river. The armies of the Marinid sultan and of our lord [Yūsuf](person:yusuf-i) lay before [Ṭarīf](place:tarifa) when the kings of Castile and Portugal came down upon them, and the camp was lost, and the women of the sultan's household with it.

The shaykh of the volunteers, [ʿUthmān b. Yaḥyā](person:uthman-b-yahya), held the ford until evening so that the sultan might withdraw, and of the men who stood with him few returned to [the war's](war:sample-war-tarifa) end.
