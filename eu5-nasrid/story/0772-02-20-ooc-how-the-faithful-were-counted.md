The figures in Book IV come from the population of every location Granada held at each checkpoint from June 1349 to September 1370, summed by culture and religion. "The valley of the great river" means the locations of the game's Andalusia area that Granada held at the time. The 1370 figures also include the north shore taken from Morocco, which is why the valley's Muslim share jumps from 19% to 25% in the last two years.

A town "turns" when the game changes its main culture or religion, and the date given is the first save that shows the change. In Qurṭuba, Muslims outnumbered Christians on 2 October 1366 (18,239 to 17,968) and became an outright majority by May 1368. The mosque-cathedral is the game's cathedral building, which Qurṭuba still has.

Invented colour: Saʿd's question about the cathedral, the Badr answer to the ʿulamāʾ, Yūsuf's answer to the nobles about Khadīja and ʿĀʾisha, and the court's unease about how Fās converted the north shore. The save shows that it did, but not how.
