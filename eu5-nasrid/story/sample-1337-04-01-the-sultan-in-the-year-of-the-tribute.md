In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. In the year seven hundred and thirty-seven, the Commander of the Muslims, [Abū al-Ḥajjāj Yūsuf](person:yusuf-i), sat upon the throne of his fathers in [Gharnāṭa](place:gharnata), the city God has set like a pearl upon the brow of the mountain. Yet the pearl paid its price: each year the gold of the realm went north to the Castilian, and each year the sultan's ministers counted what remained.

The chamberlain [Riḍwān](person:ridwan) said that a tribute paid is a war postponed, and that a postponed war is a gift of time. The sultan answered that time is a gift only to the one who uses it, and he sent to [Mālaqa](place:malaqa) for shipwrights.
