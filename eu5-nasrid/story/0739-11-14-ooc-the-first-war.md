The save has the casus belli against Aragon, dated the day of the save; the declaration, the host and Tunis taking Murcia are Trent's account (the save credits the occupied Murcia area to Morocco, the war leader). The last line lets the chronicle's narrator be Ibn al-Khaṭīb himself, as the real one was; easy to cut if it shouldn't be.
