The save has the casus belli against Aragon, dated the day of the save; the declaration, the host and Tunis taking Murcia are Trent's account (the save credits the occupied Murcia area to Morocco, the war leader). The host's figures follow the correction from the 2 September 1339 save (about 9,890 mutaṭawwiʿa, 1,200 regulars and 1,200 mercenaries), not Trent's first count.
