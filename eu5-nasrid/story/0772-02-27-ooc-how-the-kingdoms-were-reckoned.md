The survey's order is the game's great-power ladder in the save of 13 September 1370, read by `tools/world_powers.py`, and the Kingdoms hall shows the same ladder at every checkpoint. The game ranks only sovereign countries, which is why Granada has no place on it in its tributary years. The Great Powers are the ranked countries above the first Regional Power: nine in 1370, seven to ten over the campaign. "Men under arms" means regulars and raised levies on the map. In peacetime most countries stand their levies down, so a zero for Castile means no army in the field, not none to raise. Egypt's 60,000 are mostly raised levies.

Invented colour: the commission and the scribes' sources, the sultan's leaf of three lines, the court's tears for Constantinople and its argument over Fās, the Genoese advice, and Cairo's restlessness (the save shows Egypt's stability low, not its cause). Bolot's father Jamāl and his kinship with Aiman come from the save.
