**What the saves show.** This note draws on an autosave of 1 February 1451 and a save of 21 December 1451.
- *Sicily:* its 22 towns are still held directly in December, and there is no new subject. Trent's account explains why: the crown, "for now". The court's debate and who argued what are invented, but they follow the princes' arcs.
- *The Holy Cities:* the Banū Lām's occupation of Mecca, Medina, Jeddah, Yanbuʿ, Khaybar and the rest shows in every save from 29 November 1450 to 21 December 1451. The August 1449 save doesn't have it. The Mamlūks' victory at al-Ukhaydir (24 July 1451) is the war's only battle that year. Trent had said the Mamlūks were failing on the Red Sea coast; the saves show how badly.
- *The treasury:* 2,812 in February, 26 in December. The building difference covers most of it: paper mills (Seville, Almería, Xàtiva, Santarém), jewellers, a library at Lisbon, a quarry at Palermo, a cannon foundry at Jaén, lumber mills and cloth. Trent's screenshot of 26 November shows paved roads under construction. Two armouries and a grand palace are gone from the count, and the save doesn't say why.
- *Stability:* it fell from 23.6 to 16.1, and the saves don't explain it either.
- *The New World:* the institution and its date come from Trent's screenshot, and "no discovery, just the entrenchment of exploration ideals" is his account.
- *The house:* the prince Aḥmad's grandson Aḥmad was born on 16 December 1451.

**Invented colour.**
- *Sicily:* the "second idea" growing as the first was put off; Ismāʿīl's and Aḥmad's arguments; the Emperor deciding by what pleased him; "for now" read differently by everyone.
- *The Holy Cities:* the Emperor taking the news hardest, and the exchange with Ismāʿīl about Cairo.
- *Xàtiva:* its paper is real history.
- *The New World:* the students, the merchants and the jurists.
