The save of 25 May 1428 was made on the day of the Jamāʿa. In it the Primacy of Nobility is already gone, noble satisfaction is at 16% (41% in August 1424) and stability is at 1. Ten armouries stand in the save, against Trent's dozen; others may still be under construction. The spies and their message are Trent's account. Galicia's conquest of Asturias is dated by Oviedo's change of owner. The Andalusī madrasa at Ishbīliya (the game's nd_adu_madrasa) went up between the saves of 1424 and 1428 and isn't in the prose yet.

The estates panel reading is still the one from 31 December 1415. That reading included the Primacy, which doubled the umarāʾ's power, so the estimated noble share of about 21% is probably too high now. A fresh estates screenshot would recalibrate it.

Invented colour: the spies' talk in markets and monasteries and the different message for each kingdom; the Emperor's speech to the Jamāʿa and the estates' votes; the umarāʾ watching the armouries go up; the clerks' opinion of Umm al-Fatḥ; the Emperor's expectation of castles in Galicia. In the islands entry: the separate register, the captains' halfway house, the mosques of the Azores towns and the pilots' sunset at Flores. Santo Antão and Fogo appear in the save as Andalusī settlements whose faith is Catholic, so the chronicle doesn't describe their faith.
