The reputation event is from Trent's screenshot of 24 June 1431. The save of 1 October 1431 holds an "embraced antagonism" bias against al-Andalus, so the Emperor embraced the name. The crusade's participants come from that save. Genoa, Siena and Aquileia joining on 3 October, with Genoa's subjects, and the two small actions of November come from the autosave of 1 December 1431, which isn't a checkpoint. The Pope is Fabrice Ricci in the game; the chronicle gives him the Italian form of his name, as the game's own localization does for other languages. The arsenals are Trent's account and his screenshot of the game's Dar al-Sina'a building. The saves don't show how many were ordered or where, and the date is May 1432 with no day.

Invented colour: what the pamphlet said beyond "ruthless warmonger", the names the dīwān offered and the Emperor's reply, Europe hearing of it within the month, the court's reason for the English king staying out, the old rivalry between the merchants of Genoa and Ishbīliya, the doge's readiness to leave the war if trade turned against him, the treasurers' reminder, and the admirals' remark.
