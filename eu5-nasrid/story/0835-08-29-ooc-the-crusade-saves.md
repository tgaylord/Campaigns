The reputation event is from Trent's screenshot of 24 June 1431. The save of 1 October 1431 holds an "embraced antagonism" bias against al-Andalus, so the Emperor embraced the name. The crusade's participants come from that save. Genoa, Siena and Aquileia joining on 3 October, with Genoa's subjects, and the two small actions of November come from the autosave of 1 December 1431, which isn't a checkpoint. The Pope is Fabrice Ricci in the game; the chronicle gives him the Italian form of his name, as the game's own localization does for other languages. The arsenals are Trent's account and his screenshot of the game's Dar al-Sina'a building. The save of 1 May 1432 shows seven under construction (Seville, Málaga, Almería, Lisbon, Valencia, Cartagena and Murcia), about 1,760 gold already paid for them, and seven of the mod's Andalusī war galleys queued at Lisbon, Murcia, Faro, Orihuela, Málaga, Seville and Almería. The same save carries the winter's 23 battles, all fought by allies and subjects, and a war score of 4 in the allies' favour. It also shows Cape Verde made a colonial nation on 1 May 1432, which Trent's account doesn't mention. Naval losses in the war tables are in ships; a fraction means ships damaged, not sunk. Khayr al-Dīn Khayyām is a character of the game's mercenary pool, Andalusī and Sunni, commanding the Campagna army.

Invented colour: what the pamphlet said beyond "ruthless warmonger", the names the dīwān offered and the Emperor's reply, Europe hearing of it within the month, the court's reason for the English king staying out, the old rivalry between the merchants of Genoa and Ishbīliya, the doge's readiness to leave the war if trade turned against him, the treasurers' reminder, the admirals' remark, and the court's mixed feelings about Khayr al-Dīn.

The trading posts entry (Book VI) is from Trent's account and the save of 1 May 1432. That save shows eight overseas trading posts under construction in Kaabu, a vassal of Mali, at Bafatá, Bintang, Bissau, Cacheu, Casamance, Diaboudior, Gambia and Tankular. It also shows a new trade office at Cairo with 22 construction stages queued, 9 more stages for Marrakesh (level 3) and 6 for Sijilmasa (level 4). None of these is in the save of 1 October 1431. The office in Genoa (level 1) is in both saves. Invented colour: the posts as wharves and stores, the treasurers' question and the Emperor's answer, and his reason for keeping the Genoa office open. The Red Sea route of the spices is history, not the save.
