Book II now runs complete from 1346 to 1370. The ten entries from 1355 on were written in one pass after the book closed, from the saves and Trent's accounts.

**Kept out of the text**
- **The tributary glitch.** At the peace of 5 April 1366 the game put Granada back under Castile as a tributary, although Trent had declared independence. He removed it with the console before 6 November 1366. The chronicle treats Granada as free from the peace. The Mamlūk alliance vanished in the same window and was most likely lost in the fix. The chronicle has it lapse and Saʿd renew it in January 1367, which the save shows.
- **The missing battles.** The game drops a war's battle records at the peace. The second Marīnid war's battles come from the saves of 18 November 1369 and the 1 July 1370 autosave, plus Trent's screenshot of the last battle at Málaga. Anything fought between July and September 1370 is lost. Granada's dead, about 13,000, are from Trent's war screen and include attrition. The 23,000 Moroccan dead are battle losses only.

**From the game, with the chronicle's reading**
- Naṣr al-Istijjī died the day the regency ended. The game says hunting accident, and Trent's account says "sickness".
- Saʿd married Duʿāʾ, his father's widow. The game allows it. The Qurʾān (al-Nisāʾ 4:22) forbids it, so the chronicle makes the ʿulamāʾ's anger (37% in May 1364, from Trent) about the marriage, and the Jamāʿa's restored jizya the price of their silence.

**Invented colour**

- The mercenaries giving way and Khālid's guard at Antequera.
