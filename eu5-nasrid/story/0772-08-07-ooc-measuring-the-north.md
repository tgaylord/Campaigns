The three realms are the game's areas in the save of 1 November 1370: the Granada area (the old kingdom), the Andalusia area (the valley), and the eleven towns taken from Morocco on 12 September 1370 (the north shore). "What the land could yield" is the save's possible tax, and "reaching the treasury" its tax: 87 of 122 in the old kingdom, 67 of 111 in the valley, 0.14 of 17 on the north shore. Ishbīliya's market as "first in all the West" is the market list in Trent's screenshot of 24 February 1371, which ranks Šbilya 1st, Fās 3rd and Niani 8th.

The Spread of Usury and the sultan's answer are the game's event and Trent's choice. Invented colour: the sultan's remarks about wheat, looms and Genoese lenders, the qāḍī reciting the verse, and the assignment of Ishbīliya to Duʿāʾ and Jayyān to Maryam (the game says only that the two are assimilating them).
