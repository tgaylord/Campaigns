Written in November 2026 at Trent's direction: the struggle between the queens is to be a central thread of Saʿd's reign. Duʿāʾ stands for the Maghrib and Aiman for Egypt, with the matriarch Sayyida against Duʿāʾ, Faraj's going opening the way for Duʿāʾ's rise, and the princes Ismāʿīl and Aḥmad bound together from childhood. Trent allowed details the saves don't hold to be invented for this. Everything below is invented colour unless marked.

**The new entry, *Of the Two Queens, in the Autumn Fās Came Again*.**
- *From the record:* the Mamlūk alliance, lost in autumn 1366 and renewed on 30 January 1367; Abū ʿInān's declaration and his defeat at Cádiz on 11 November 1368; the ages of the children.
- *Invented:* the whispers about Duʿāʾ's letters; Aiman writing to Cairo and her envoy bringing the treaty home; Duʿāʾ's jewels, her scholars preaching the jihad, and the heir shown at the gate; Sayyida's words to the court; and Saʿd's saying about keeping the Maghrib and Egypt both.

**Passages added to earlier entries.**
- *The Governor of Qurṭuba (1355):* Faraj going gladly, the Saharan guides and his talk of the salt roads; Sayyida's remark on the harem law.
- *The Great Madrasa (1358):* Sayyida's court in the harem, and the scholars not asked to it.
- *The Regent Who Did Not Last (1364):* Sayyida's year of silence, and Aiman writing to Cairo.
- *The Peace of Independence (1366):* the two parties of the palace.
- *Duʿāʾ's Regency (1374):* the perfect courtesy between Duʿāʾ and Sayyida.
- *The Restoration (1375):* the exchange between Duʿāʾ and Aiman.
- *The Kingdom of Ṭulayṭula (1380):* the order of the wives behind Sayyida's bier.
- *The Death of Duʿāʾ (1388):* Aiman's white mourning.
- *The Western Ocean (1392):* the guides who knew Faraj's name.
- *The Sons of Muḥammad (1408):* the two boys never apart, Ismāʿīl talking and Aḥmad drawing towns in the sand.
- *The Two Princes (1429):* the division of the work between them, and Aḥmad to preside over Ismāʿīl's council.
- *The Closed Ports (1447):* Aḥmad as the hand that spent the treasury on the towns (Trent's direction), with the building itself from the saves.
