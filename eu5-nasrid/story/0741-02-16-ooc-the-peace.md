From the saves: the estate loans and the crown's fall to about a seventh (estimated, not read off the screen), the fall of Uryūla, the Tlemcenī garrison destroyed at Murcia, both battles of Uryūla with their numbers, the thirteen towns ceded, the reparations to 1350, the treasury, the population and the two armies' positions on the day of the peace. From Trent's account: the road home being cut, the hunger, Jumilla's fall date, the army sizes behind the bluff (about 6,000 against 16,000; the save's two armies come to 15,726), and the threat to burn Barcelona that the host could probably not have made good past Xàtiva. The dhimmī share after the conquest is the estimator's least certain number: culture penalties for the new Catalan and Aragonese subjects aren't modelled, so it may run high.
