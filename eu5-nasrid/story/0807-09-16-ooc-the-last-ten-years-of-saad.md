The six entries from August 1395 to March 1405 were written together during the chronicle sweep of November 2026. Their facts come from the saves and Trent's screens as logged in the events they cite. Saʿd's character follows his traits in the 1405 save (conqueror, entrepreneur, hardy warrior, incorruptible), and the "more honest place" is the game's death notice. Trent's reading, that the Conqueror became the nation's greatest builder, is the thread of the obituary.

Invented colour: the Emperor's remarks (the lance and the pen, the crossroads town, the assayers "paid to find", the merchant who tells the market, the Pope's city as a lesson); the chancery's pay and its ban on gifts; Ismāʿīl reading the letters to Paris; the Emperor riding out to the first water at Qarmūna; his refusal to hand the army to a general in 806; the crown prince's fair words to his nephew; and the soldiers' words at Moya. The game gives no detail of how Avignon fell or how Saʿd died beyond the place.
