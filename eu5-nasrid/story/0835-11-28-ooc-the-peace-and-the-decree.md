The peace terms are from Trent's screenshot, and the save of 27 July 1432 was taken just before the peace. There's no save after it, because no land changed hands. The war score in that save is 5 in the defenders' favour. The peace screen's gold line read +114.64, and the chronicle doesn't put a figure on the reparations. The war with Mantua and Milan (started 6 March 1432, against Faenza, with the Pope and Genoa defending) and the late joining of Friuli and Lower Carinthia are from the save. The decree on the ṭāʾifas is Trent's account and has no save of its own. It is dated to the day of the peace, and the order may have been given a little later.

Invented colour: the court's satisfaction at Christendom's two wars, the Emperor rereading the Mondego report, the Pope's envoys coming to Ishbīliya, the purpose behind the treaty clause, the name "Peace of Dhū al-Qaʿda", the louder complaints in the northern ṭāʾifas, and the old men of the dīwān at the end. Who complained, and how loudly, is Trent's "more than a little grumbling", given a shape.
