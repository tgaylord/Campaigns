Trent's verdict on the Mamlūk war: super quick. The war ran from 19 September 1423 to 8 August 1424, and the save of 14 August 1424 no longer keeps its battle list, so the chronicle doesn't claim any. The peace terms come from the 37 towns that changed hands between the saves of November 1423 and August 1424. The war score is reckoned from the length of the truce: about 65 against the Ottomans and about 35 against Nafusa.

The marriages aren't dated in the save. They were made between September 1420 and January 1422, when the first children were born, and the event is dated to the first save that shows them. The brides' fathers come from the save: Tella's is Abū Ḥammū of Fās, Ayaulym's Aqṭāy of Egypt, Khadīja's the Ottoman sultan Aḥmad, and Dīma's Abū Ḥammū of the Ḥafṣids. The Kingdom of Portugal's Catholic state faith and its king's Islam are also from the save.

Invented colour: the coronation before the bishops; the king's mosque at Barcelos; the court's message to Cairo; the Mamlūk advance through Cilicia; the letters of congratulation and condolence on one ship; and the preachers on Pedro's stairs. The game gives his death as a fall down stairs, and the chronicle takes it at its word.
