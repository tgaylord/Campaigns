The battle numbers are the save's own: each side's men and losses, read out of the war's battle records in the saves of 14 September 1347, 1 July 1348 and 27 January 1349. The save doesn't record how much war score a battle moved, so the rule that only battles worth 5 or more get prose was applied by size. Every land battle Granada fought with more than about 3,000 enemy losses is in the text. The naval losses and the allies' defeats are summed up in a sentence each. The Volunteers' return in 1346 is told in-world, as Trent asked. Trent's own reading drives the turns of the story: the doubt about joining the war, the Moroccans "throwing away war score", the plague breaking Castile's morale before the peace, and the surprise that Castile accepted the terms. The Castilian levies deserting, the guards drawn away at al-Manṣūra and Süleyman Bey's courtesy are the chronicle's colour. The Ottoman reply is quoted from the event.
