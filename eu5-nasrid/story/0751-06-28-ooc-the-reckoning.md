The dīwān's reckoning compares the same lands in two saves, 1 August 1346 (the day the Black Death rose at Yaik) and 2 September 1350. Each location's population is the sum of its pops. "The old lands of Gharnāṭa" means the 1346 realm only. Lands won since aren't counted, so conquest doesn't hide the losses. The city-against-countryside split uses the game's own rank for each location (city, town or rural settlement), and the losses by estate use each pop's type. The worse-hit realm "far in the East" is the tag KUT, the only country of over 300,000 people that lost a larger share (−47.8%). The panel figures (221,000 dead in the realm, 47.46 million in the world, 4 locations still affected) are from Trent's screenshot of the same day.
