On the first day of the feast, as our lord [Yūsuf](person:yusuf-i) bowed in the last prostration, a madman of the city drew a blade and struck him, and the Commander of the Muslims was carried to the palace and did not see the evening. The people tore the murderer apart and burned what remained.

That same night the chamberlain [Riḍwān](person:ridwan) brought the boy [Muḥammad](person:muhammad-v) before the shaykhs, and they gave him the oath, and he was sixteen years old.
