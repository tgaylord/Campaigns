Praise be to God, who returns to the faithful what the faithful had lent to others. [Runda](place:rundah), the eagle's nest above its gorge, which the sons of Marīn had held for two generations, opened its gates to our lord [al-Ghanī bi-llāh](person:muhammad-v) after a winter of siege.

The vizier [Lisān al-Dīn](person:ibn-al-khatib) composed for the occasion a qaṣīda of sixty lines, which the sultan ordered carved upon the gate, and which this humble chronicler, being its author, will not presume to praise.
