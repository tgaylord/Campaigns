How this page works (mockup notes)
Text
The prose and the verse only. The running head shrinks to the date and the reign.
Names
Left margin: each person, place, realm and court term is glossed beside the line where it first appears, as of this page's own date (ages, offices from the nearest reckoning, what the record shows before this day, never after). Tap a later mention to open its gloss inline under the paragraph.
Full
Adds the right margin: the dated record the entry draws on, in ledger type, beside the sentence it supports, with a provenance mark. Click a record note to see why it is anchored there. Records that could not be anchored with confidence sit at the foot under “Drawn on”, with the reason.
