# Glossed Folio: wireframes

All text in these frames is real: it comes from the chronicle entries, `data/`, and the build in this folder. The only exception is the player's note, which is a placeholder because the real notes aren't in brief2/. Widths are indicative: desktop is about 1240px of paper, phone is 390px.

Key:
`¹ ²`: record superscripts (Full only)
`‥‥`: dotted underline on a first mention
`[ ]`: the reserved slot owned by another designer
`＋`: a compacted gloss, which opens on click

**How the crowd is handled:**
1. **First mentions only.** ¶1 alone introduces 13 names, but no name is glossed twice.
2. **Shrinking.** A gloss pushed more than about 150px below its line shrinks to one line with ＋. Clicking it opens it in place and lays the margin out again.
3. **Records stay in text order,** so they always run downward with the prose, even when they drift. Hovering a slip highlights its sentence.
4. **When in doubt, don't anchor.** The four new ṭāʾifas tie between two sentences, so the record goes to the foot with its reason.
5. **The realm at the close** says what the prose implies: the conquests went to the ṭāʾifas (5 → 9), not to the crown (161 → 161).

At 40+ sources a further rule would group slips that share a paragraph into one slip with a count.
