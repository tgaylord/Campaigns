The ships came first. The realm's heart followed a generation later

From 1379 the court built a fleet, but the temper of the realm, the slow drift between land and sea, did not move for half a century. Then it moved 63 points in thirteen years.

The realm begins leaning a little toward the land, at −10 on a scale from −100 (the land) to +100 (the sea). Its navy is five ships: two galleys, two cogs and a caravel.
