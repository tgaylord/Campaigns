HOW TO READ A COIN OF AL-ANDALUS

SIZE
Land. Area grows with the locations held: 25, 50, 100, 200. Same scale for every reign.
METAL
fine: holds at least what it owes
pale: owes more than it holds
base: owes over five times as much
blank: nothing recorded
PUNCHES
One square countermark for each war under way.
WORDS
The square: the Naṣrid motto over the ruler's name. Around it: title (top), mint (bottom), Hijri year (left), Western year (right).
REVERSE
Flip for the ledger. Stippled = estimated. Estate notches ▲▼ appear only when an estate has moved off the level the crown holds it at.

Coins are generated from checkpoints.json; all 72 saves, 1337–1442. Hijri dates computed with the tabular calendar (game dates read as Julian). Coins are struck for this chronicle, not replicas. Mockup: low fidelity.
