# Rome campaign — fact book from the save files

*Built from 26 saves: the seven readable ones (Oct 1349, May 1365, May 1369, Apr 1428, Mar 1437, Aug 1500, Jan 1507) and 19 binary saves decoded for this (1393 to 1506). The decoding was checked against two readable saves taken days apart from binary ones (Feb/Mar 1437 and Aug 1500), and matched on land, population, rulers, subjects, laws and every war record. Everything here is read from the files; anything inferred is marked **(inferred)**, and anything you told me is marked **(your account)**. Names are the game's internal keys, tidied up, except where you have renamed something.*

## At a glance

- **Started** 1 April 1337 as Byzantium (BYZ). Became a great power on 1 October 1358. Now **Rome (ROM)**, ranked **#1** among great powers.
- **Population** of the directly ruled realm: 1.22M in 1337 → 23.14M in 1507. Subjects hold roughly another 20.6M.
- **Tax base**: 46 in 1337 → 5894 in 1507. Monthly income 6,417, treasury 49,592.
- **Territory**: 637 locations held directly, plus 30 subjects (643 locations).
- **Capital**: Constantinople throughout.
- **Religion**: Orthodox at the start; **Hellenism** was re-established on 1 February 1410 and is the state faith. In 1507 the realm is 36% Hellenist, 31% Sunni, 14% Orthodox and 14% Catholic.
- **Culture**: Greek primary. Accepted: Venetian, Bulgarian, Armenian. Tolerated: Aromanian, Roman.
- **Hegemonies**: military and cultural since 1 Jan 1437, economic since 1 Mar 1445, diplomatic since 1 Jun 1492. France holds naval hegemony (since 1503).
- **Rivals** (date declared): Tunis (1368), Persia/IRA (1423), Spain (1461), France (1485).
- **Disasters survived**: the Byzantine Succession Crisis and the Fate of the Phoenix (won).

Notes from the records:
- Every emperor from Demetrius to Philip III died in service: two at sea as admirals, then on the march, at a siege, on the march again, and in camp.
- Crown Prince **Anastasius** (born 1395, heir in 1428 and 1437) died in a **hunting accident at Constantinople on 27 June 1437**. His younger brother Alexis became heir and succeeded in 1439.
- **Alexis VI** is venerated as a Hellenist saint (a "god-emperor") of the realm.
- **Empresses consort** as recorded in the saves: Theodora (Demetrius I); Helen Komnene (Philip II); **Desislava** of the Bulgarian Sratsimir house (John V); **Esclarmonde**, a Catalan Catholic (Basil III); **Zabava**, a Novgorodian (Alexis VI); **Anthousa** Karantene (Philip III); Theodora-Mary Palaiologina, then **Eudokia** Adralestene of Sicily by June 1501 (Nicholas I).
- **Heirs**: Philip (to 1356), John (to 1389), Basil (by 1393), Anastasius (by 1421, killed 1437), Philip (by 1442), Nicholas (by 1460), Theophilos (by 1473).
- John V is recorded as Orthodox at his death, even though Hellenism was re-established during his reign; Basil III is the first emperor recorded as Hellenist.
